# Spice&Soul

Spice&Soul is a restaurant website for browsing the menu, viewing the photo album, booking a table, and getting in touch.

## Pages

* Home — hero, about, specials, and testimonials (`src/index.html`)
* Menu — Food, Desserts, and Drinks (`src/pages/food.html`, `desserts.html`, `drinks.html`)
* About — history and job openings (`src/pages/about.html`)
* Album and galleries — food, desserts, drinks, location, and staff (`src/pages/album.html` and `src/pages/gallery-*.html`)
* Reservations (`src/pages/reservations.html`)
* Contact (`src/pages/contact.html`)

## Preview

Open `src/index.html` in a browser. The `dist/` and `docs/` folders are built copies of the same site.
